Merge duplicate products

Example: two Gemini products from different providers → keep one, merge the other. Sold accounts show on the keep product only.

What happens
  • All orders / sold history from the duplicate move to the keep product
  • Stock login lines + qty merge into the keep product
  • User discounts & sales move when possible
  • Duplicate is hidden (soft-deleted) and unlinked from its provider
  • SKU / name of the keep product stay the same
